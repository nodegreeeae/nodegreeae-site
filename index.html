FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need a degree?

No. I don't have one. Most SDR postings say "bachelor's preferred" and most SDR floors are full of people without one. A referral from someone on the team makes that line disappear.

I've never sold anything. Does this still work?

Yes, it takes a couple more weeks. We build your story first and your list leans toward companies that hire in classes and train from zero. You show up having already talked to people on the team.

Will you do the outreach for me?

No, and that's the point. I write every message and fix them every week, but you send them. By day 90 you have the job and the skill that keeps it.

What if I don't have an offer in 90 days?

I keep working with you free for up to 6 more months, until you sign. The deal: you're doing the outreach. A week without messages pauses the clock, and if you turn down an offer from a company on your list, the guarantee ends. I can't want it more than you do. Results vary, no specific job or income is guaranteed.

How much time does it take?

60-90 minutes of outreach a day, plus the interviews. Works around a full-time job. Doesn't work around skipping days.

Why not just apply online?

Because 300 other people did. A good SDR posting gets hundreds of applications in a week and the recruiter reads the referred ones first. Applying is what you do after someone on the team already said your name.
