FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a degree?+

No. I don't have one. Most SDR postings say "bachelor's preferred" and most SDR floors are full of people who don't have one. A referral from someone on the team makes that line disappear.

I've never sold anything. Does this still work?+

Yes, it just takes a couple more weeks. We build your story first, then your target list leans toward companies that hire in classes and train from zero. You'll be the one who shows up having already talked to three people on the team.

What if I don't have an offer in 90 days?+

I keep working with you for free until you do. The one condition is that you're doing the outreach. If you go a week without sending messages, the clock pauses and so does the guarantee. I can't want it more than you do.

How much time does this take?+

60 to 90 minutes a day of outreach, a 30-minute call with me every week, and the interviews themselves. It works around a full-time job. It does not work around skipping days.

Where do you place people?+

Any US metro. I build the list around where you are and how far you'll commute, and I include remote roles where they're real. Your list is 15 to 20 companies with an open role right now, not a generic list of logos.

Why not just apply online?+

Because 300 other people did. An SDR posting at a good company gets hundreds of applications in a week and the recruiter reads the referred ones first. Applying is what you do after someone on the team already said your name.

What's the fit call?+

15 minutes. I ask where you are, what you've tried, and what you want. If I can get you hired I'll tell you how. If I can't, I'll tell you that too and point you somewhere better.
