FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a degree?+

No. I don't have one. Most SDR postings say "bachelor's preferred" and most SDR floors are full of people without one. A referral from someone on the team makes that line disappear.

I've never sold anything. Does this still work?+

Yes, it takes a couple more weeks. We build your story first and your list leans toward companies that hire in classes and train from zero. You show up having already talked to three people on the team.

Will you do the outreach for me?+

No, and that's the point. Services that message hiring managers from your account hand you an interview and nothing else. You walk onto an SDR floor and have to do the thing you never learned, with a quota. I write every message and fix them every week, but you send them. By day 90 you have the job and the skill that keeps it.

What if I don't have an offer in 90 days?+

I keep working with you free until you do. One condition: you're doing the outreach. A week without messages pauses the clock and the guarantee. I can't want it more than you do.

How much time does it take?+

60-90 minutes of outreach a day, a 30-minute call with me every week, and the interviews. Works around a full-time job. Doesn't work around skipping days.

Why not just apply online?+

Because 300 other people did. A good SDR posting gets hundreds of applications in a week and the recruiter reads the referred ones first. Applying is what you do after someone on the team already said your name.
